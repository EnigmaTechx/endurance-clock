Keys & gestures

M
Miller’s planet: one hour here, seven years there
W
Murph’s watch: the second hand ticks STAY in Morse
A
Alarm and countdown panel
T
Tick sound on or off
L
About the current hour’s module; ← → step round the ring
C
Start or stop the camera tour
R
Reset the view
F
Fullscreen
?
This help; Esc closes anything
Drag
Tilt the ship; move the mouse to lean it
Tap
A module, the hub or the Ranger for its story
Wait
Leave it alone and the camera takes a tour of the ship
On the hour
The Ranger undocks and flies to the next module
Menu
On phones the modes sit under Menu
